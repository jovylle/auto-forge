        How to play

        1. Drag blobs onto empty tiles. Districts drift on their own each tide — anchor them where the soil is rich (◍ pips).
        2. Tap blob → blob (or drag blob onto blob) to draw a trade route (max 6). Routes pay every tide: goods + soil − distance. Tap a route line to cut it.
        3. Fog hides soil. Districts reveal the 8 tiles around them; routes light their path. Pulse ◉ burns 3◦ to reveal far tiles (cooldown 2 tides).
        4. Win: reach quota before tide 20 ends. ★ Daily seed: same map for everyone today — beat it, keep the streak.
