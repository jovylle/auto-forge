番台の約束 rules

  1. Connect entrance 繋 (top) to exit 巣 (bottom).
  2. Every relay 中継 cell must glow in the same circuit.
  3. Broken 故障 cells can never light.
  4. Tap a cell to light / snuff it. The current flows only through touching lit cells.

    遊び方 — how to play

    静かに、灯をともす。
    Light the comb, and let the signal find its way home.

    1. Tap hex cells to switch them on / off.
    2. Golden cells are energized — they touch the entrance through other lit cells.
    3. You win when the exit and every relay ◆ are all energized at once.
    4. Dark cracked cells are broken — route around them.
    5. Fewer moves than par earns up to three stars ★.

    Tip: relays are the puzzle. Connect them first, then run one trunk down to the exit.
