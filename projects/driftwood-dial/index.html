— Captain's Log —

Each chapter is a leg of the voyage. Scroll a chapter into view and the dial answers the helm.

I

Departure from Cinder Quay

Leg 1 · ordered heading 045° · full sail, boiler banked

We cast off at first bell, the dial trembling to north-east. Gulls like torn proclamations overhead. The driftwood helm — salvaged from a wreck older than the lighthouse — turns smooth as buttered clockwork.

II

Cross-currents of the Reach

Leg 2 · ordered heading 120° · current abeam, mind the set

By noon the easterly current takes us by the shoulder and walks us sideways. The needle argues with the compass; the wise captain splits the difference and lets the dial do the sums.

III

Threading the Brass Narrows

Leg 3 · ordered heading 210° · slow ahead, reefs both sides

Reefs grin either side like brass teeth. Throttle eased to five knots, breath held, eyes on the dotted line. The computer has never lied to me yet — though it has, once, been economical with the truth.

IV

Landfall at Driftwood Light

Leg 4 · ordered heading 300° · stoke the boiler, homeward

Full steam at last. The light swings round — there! — and every scroll of the log has sailed us closer. Mark the landfall bearing in the plaque above, sign the log, and export it for the Admiralty.
