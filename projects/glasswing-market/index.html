How does the market work?
  1. Buy wing-goods from the glass stalls. Prices drift every day — the arrow shows today’s trend.
  2. Haggle once per stall per day: charm the keeper for lower prices, or risk annoying them.
  3. Sell from your satchel when a stall pays well, or fill villager orders for coins + renown.
  4. Ring the bell to open the next market day. After day 7, your final market score is tallied.
