あそびかた — how to play
  1. Press 開店 OPEN SHOP to start the 90-second day.
  2. Baskets pile up on the counter. Click an empty machine (あき) to load the next basket — costs 1 coin.
  3. While a machine washes, tap it to agitate (−0.3s per tap). Listen for the bubbles.
  4. When a machine flashes できあがり DONE, click to collect: +2 coins, +10 pts. Fast collections build a combo 連.
  5. Nettle イガ loads take longer but pay +4 coins, +20 pts. Out of coins? The next load is on the house (おまけ).
