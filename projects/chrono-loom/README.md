# Chrono Loom

A weaving clock where each hour threads a new color into a living tapestry. Vaporwave aesthetic.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in any modern browser (works from `file://` and static hosts). No build, no dependencies.

## What it does
- Each "hour" weaves a new colored weft row into the tapestry (auto every ~2.5s, or click WEAVE +1).
- Scroll/drag on the canvas to pan through the tapestry history.
- History ribbon shows every woven hour; click a swatch to jump.
- Space toggles auto-weave; RESET clears; PNG exports the canvas; SHARE copies a link encoding the tapestry.

Playable in under 30 seconds: open, watch it weave, scroll, share.
