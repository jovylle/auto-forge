stratum 01 · foundation

The foundress chews the first carton.

Low, slow, almost subsonic. The first ring of amber hexagons goes up around the stalk and the whole tower breathes at one cycle per second. Keep scrolling — the spire camera climbs with you.

stratum 02 · brood comb

Sorrel-capped brood cells hum back.

Every fifth cell is capped in rust-red sorrel pulp. Capped cells resonate: the drone gains its buzzing upper partial, and wasps orbit faster the quicker you scroll. Flick the page and feel them startle.

stratum 03 · crown

The crown narrows toward the light.

Rings taper as the tower climbs. The hum brightens with altitude — the filter opens, the wings blur. At the crown you can sting the air (a pitch-zap) or freeze the whole swarm as an image.
