keyboard-only field guide

  • Tab — reach everything. Every knob is a real slider, every tape a real button.
  • P — power the lagoon on/off  ·  1–4 — toggle tape I–IV
  • ← → — select tape  ·  [ / ] (or -/=) — pitch-bend selected ∓1 semitone (Shift = fine)
  • , / . — volume of selected deck  ·  0 all on · 9 all off · C still water
  • E — export 8s WAV mix  ·  M — mute master  ·  H or ? — this card
  • lagoon focused: arrows move splash cursor, Enter/Space splash + toggle nearest tape, drag buoys with pointer for pitch.
