    How to yodel

    1. Goal: sing each note of the phrase in order. Waking all 8 herbs finishes the round.
    2. No mic? Hold HOLD TO YODEL and drag up/down, move the yodel pitch slider, or tap the herb keys (A–G).
    3. Mic? Press enable mic, then hum or yodel. Chrome/Edge over https or localhost work best.
    4. Yodel ×2: wobble your pitch wide (or hold Space) while hitting a note for double points.
    5. Land inside the glowing zone on the meter. Green-gold burst = perfect (±25¢).
