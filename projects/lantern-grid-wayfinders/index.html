    How to play

    1. Press + drag (or tap-drag) starting on a lit lantern — the big glowing hex.
    2. Chain through adjacent hexes. Max trail charge: 14 hexes.
    3. Release on / next to an unlit outpost to relight it. Chain outward from lit ones.
    4. Fog tiles (static noise) are unwalkable and rewrite districts. Lit rails are immune.
    5. Relight all 12 outposts to dawn the grid and win.
