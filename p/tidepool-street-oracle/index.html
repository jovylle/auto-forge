II. The Drowning Prophecy

Three readings. As you descend, the sea rises — if the tide is scroll-bound.

☽ low water

First reading — the cobbles remember

At low tide every alley holds. Ghosts walk the long way round, past lantern-blocks and sleeping courts. Sketch generously; the town rewards the bold.

☾ half tide

Second reading — the shallows bargain

Half-drowned crossings flicker. Watch the pale ones hesitate, then swerve — a live reroute, mid-step. Raise a causeway with the ▲ raise brush to save a drowning lane.

● high water

Third reading — the oracle keeps what it kisses

At full flood only the high roads survive. Stranded walkers gutter like candles. Export a postcard of the ruin — proof you were warned.
