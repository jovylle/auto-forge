    How to work the Observatory

    Everything works with keyboard only. The sky canvas is focusable — Tab to it, then use arrows. All buttons are real buttons. The logbook is a real list. Screen readers get an aria-live toast on every action.

    • Pin: press N, write, press Ctrl+Enter. Or click the sky.
    • Wander: ←→↑↓ jumps to the nearest star in that direction.
    • Share: S copies a link with your whole sky encoded in the URL hash. Send it; the sky rebuilds on open.
    • Sound: all tones are synthesized WebAudio — no files. M mutes.
