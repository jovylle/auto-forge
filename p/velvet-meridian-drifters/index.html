❓ SKIPPER'S HANDBOOK

How to play

  1. On your turn, press & drag across orthogonally-connected open tiles. Max 6 per turn (Spyglass gives +3).
  2. Your trail must touch your color (after your first claim). Blocked 〰 reefs break trails.
  3. Star-charts ★ = 3 pts, plain tiles = 1 pt, anything on the meridian = ×2.
  4. ⚡ tiles grant a random power-up. Use them from the side panel anytime on your turn.
  5. Gust: tap any open tile → claims the 3×3 around it. Anchor: your next 3 claimed tiles lock 🔒 (can't be storm-stolen). Spyglass: +3 drag-moves immediately.
  6. Every few turns a storm steals an unlocked edge tile back to the sea — anchors stop it!
  7. 12 rounds total (12 turns each). Highest points wins. Daily seed = same chart for everyone that day.
