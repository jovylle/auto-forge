飛脚 MESSAGE

Press any move key to begin your run.

Every step spends ink — pine forest and shallows cost 2, open washi costs 1. Stamping a checkpoint refunds +12 ink and claims the land around it. Stranded at 0 ink? Restart keeps the same fog.

使い方 HOW TO

Six neighbours, six keys. Top row Q W E reaches north-west, north-east, east. Bottom row A S D reaches west, south-west, south-east. Arrow keys cover four of the six; Q / E cover the diagonals. Grey hexes are unrevealed fog. Red ○ marks are unclaimed checkpoints — step on one to stamp it. Claim all four to complete the atlas.
