    How it works

    Every frame is recorded into a 10-second ring buffer. Press R to freeze time and scrub the slider backwards — player position, shards, score and combo are restored. Clocks keep their patrol but you keep your knowledge. 3 charges per run; every 8-shard combo streak earns +1.

    Mazes are procedural (recursive backtracker) from the seed. The daily seed is — same maze for everyone today.
