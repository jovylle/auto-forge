Plant

Tap or click the soil. Each flower gets its own clock loop — it blooms, fades, and blooms again on its own period.

Loops

Watch blooms follow clock loops. Petals open with the hand; when the loop closes, it restarts.

Time

Speed up time to watch seasons of bloom in seconds. Slow it to study a single loop.

Share

Copy a garden seed to the URL. Anyone opening it grows the same garden.

Keyboard

  • ↑ ↓ ← → move planting cursor
  • Enter / Space plant a flower
  • + / - speed up / slow time
  • S share seed · C clear · Esc stop
  • Scroll to pan the garden view
