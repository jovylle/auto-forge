一

Walk to paint

Each step presses moss into the stone. Keep moving — every tile you touch glows, and your territory is the sum of everywhere your feet have lately been.

二

Link the beacons

Five stone beacons sleep in the fog. Wake each one by stepping onto it, then keep your moss-path unbroken between them. Connected beacons hum as one route — pairs of linked beacons score fifty stones each, multiplied by your combo.

三

Outpace the fog

Moss left unwalked withers. Fog creeps back over idle tiles, so circle back to refresh your trail. Scrolling stirs the wind: a brisk scroll slows the fog's appetite; still air lets it hunger. The mountain is new every day — one shared seed for everyone, midnight to midnight.
