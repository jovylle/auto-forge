Gardener’s Chronicle — scroll to stoke the boiler

Scrolling this page raises boiler pressure: the master filter opens, the fog thins, and the gears spin faster. Scroll back down to drowse the garden into a low muffled hum.

I. Sowing

Every reel is wound by hand in the potting shed. Bass Reels root deepest; Chime Spools flower only when crossfaded against their neighbours.

II. Training the stems

A stem pulled skyward sings sharper; one trained toward the evening window drifts right of ear. Prune without mercy — the compost remembers every rhythm.

III. On rain

Rain is the garden’s editor. It rusts some steps shut and oils others open. Purists cover their reels; the wise leave them out and transcribe what the storm writes.

IV. The phonograph

One tap of the ruby stylus engraves whatever the wind is playing. Carry the wax cylinder home, or post the garden’s coordinates to a friend — their bed will bloom exactly as yours stood.
